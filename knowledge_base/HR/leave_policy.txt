# Employee Leave Policy

This document defines the types of leave available to employees and the procedures for requesting time off.

## Types of Leave

### 1. Annual Leave (Vacation)
* Full-time employees accrue annual leave at a rate of 20 days per calendar year.
* Accrual occurs monthly (1.67 days per month).
* A maximum of 5 unused annual leave days can be carried over to the following calendar year. Any additional unused days beyond 5 will be forfeited on December 31st.

### 2. Sick Leave
* Employees receive 10 paid sick leave days per year, allotted on January 1st (or pro-rated for mid-year starts).
* Sick leave is intended for personal illness or medical appointments.
* Medical certification is required for sick leave absences exceeding 3 consecutive business days.
* Sick leave days do not carry over to the next year and are not paid out upon separation.

### 3. Bereavement Leave
* Paid bereavement leave is available in the event of the death of an immediate family member:
  - Spouse, child, or parent: Up to 5 consecutive business days.
  - Sibling or grandparent: Up to 3 consecutive business days.

## Request and Approval Process

To request leave, follow these steps:

1. **Submit Request in HR Portal**:
   - Log in to the HR Self-Service portal at `https://hr.generic-enterprise.com`.
   - Select the leave type and enter the start and end dates.
   - For planned vacation, requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

2. **Manager Approval**:
   - The system will route the request to your manager.
   - Managers will review based on department coverage and projects, and approve or decline.

3. **Calendar Update**:
   - Once approved, set your Out-of-Office (OOO) calendar event and set up auto-reply messages in email and chat.
