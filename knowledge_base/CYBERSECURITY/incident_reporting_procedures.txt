# Cybersecurity Incident Reporting Procedures

This document describes how and when employees must report suspected cybersecurity incidents or anomalies.

## What is a Security Incident?
A security incident is any event that threatens the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of corporate systems or data. Examples include:
* Active phishing attempts or clicking on a suspected phishing link.
* Unexplained system behavior or signs of unauthorized access (e.g., cursor moving independently, unauthorized password changes).
* Loss or theft of a corporate-managed device (laptop, phone).
* Unauthorized disclosure or leaks of sensitive documents or data.
* Finding unauthorized physical devices connected to the network or workstations.

## Reporting Steps

If you suspect or identify a security incident, execute the following steps immediately:

1. **Do Not Attempt to Investigate**:
   - Do not attempt to clean infected systems or run unauthorized scanners yourself.
   - Do not shut down the device unless specifically instructed by Security (restarting or shutting down can destroy volatile evidence in memory). Simply disconnect from Wi-Fi or unplug the network cable if malware is active.

2. **Notify the Security Operations Center (SOC)**:
   - Email: Send details to `security-report@generic-enterprise.com`.
   - Hotline: Call the Security Incident line at extension 9110.
   - Portal: Submit an "Incident Report" ticket on the support homepage.

3. **Provide Critical Details**:
   - Include a description of what occurred or what was observed.
   - Date and time of the occurrence.
   - Hostname of the affected device (found on the asset tag sticker).
   - Any screenshots of error messages, phishing emails, or suspicious activities.

## Post-Report Guidance
* Follow instructions provided by the security response team.
* Keep the incident confidential to prevent tip-offs if an active compromise is investigated.
* Coordinate with the IT team for device isolation or replacement if requested.
