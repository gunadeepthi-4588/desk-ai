# Security Awareness Basics (NIST-Aligned)

This document outlines the core security practices aligned with standard NIST (National Institute of Standards and Technology) guidelines for maintaining workplace cybersecurity.

## Workstation Security
Securing your physical and digital workstation is the first line of defense against unauthorized access:

1. **Lock Your Screen**:
   - Always lock your computer screen when leaving your desk, even if only for a brief moment.
   - Shortcut on Windows: Press `Win + L`.
   - Shortcut on macOS: Press `Ctrl + Cmd + Q`.

2. **Clean Desk Policy**:
   - Do not leave sensitive papers, notes containing passwords, or storage media (like USB drives) unattended on your desk.
   - Lock physical documents in drawers or cabinets when not in use.

3. **Secure Workstation Settings**:
   - Automatic screen lock is enforced after 10 minutes of inactivity. Do not attempt to disable this setting.

## Device and Data Management
* **Approved Software Only**: Install software only from the official software center. Unapproved third-party software poses a security risk.
* **USB and External Storage**: Do not connect unknown USB drives or external storage media to your workstation. 
* **Data Encryption**: Ensure BitLocker (Windows) or FileVault (macOS) is enabled on your laptop storage drive.
* **Workplace Shredding**: Discard physical documents containing sensitive or proprietary data into the designated secure shredding bins.
