# Purchase Order and Invoice Processing Guide

This document details the procurement process, including creating purchase orders (POs) and submitting vendor invoices for payment.

## Purchase Order (PO) Creation
A Purchase Order must be approved and issued before purchasing goods or services exceeding **$1,000**:

1. **Submit Request**: Log in to the purchasing system and complete a purchase requisition form. Specify the vendor details, item descriptions, quantities, and cost center.
2. **Review & Approval**:
   - Requisitions up to $5,000: Approved by the department manager.
   - Requisitions over $5,000: Approved by the department VP.
3. **PO Issuance**: Once approved, the system generates a PO number and emails the Purchase Order to the vendor.

## Invoice Submission and Processing
When a vendor invoice is received, follow these steps to ensure timely payment:

1. **Verify the Invoice**:
   - Confirm that the goods or services have been received or completed satisfactorily.
   - Verify that the invoice references the correct corporate PO number (required for invoices over $1,000).

2. **Submit to Accounts Payable**:
   - Email the PDF invoice to `ap@generic-enterprise.com` or upload it directly through the procurement system portal.
   - The invoice must include the vendor's billing address, tax identification number, and bank routing info.

3. **Processing and Payment Terms**:
   - Standard payment terms are **Net 30** (payment is made 30 days from the invoice receipt date).
   - Invoices without a valid PO number (where required) will be held and returned to the department for matching, which will delay processing.
