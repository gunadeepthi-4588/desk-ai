# Expense Reimbursement Procedure

This document outlines the guidelines and procedures for submitting business expenses for reimbursement.

## Eligible Expenses

### 1. Business Travel Meals
* Meals are reimbursed on an actual expense basis up to a daily limit of **$75.00**.
* Alcohol is not eligible for reimbursement unless pre-approved for client entertainment events.
* Itemized receipts are required for all meal claims. Summary credit card slips are not accepted.

### 2. Client Entertainment
* Reimbursed only when client names, company name, and business purpose are clearly documented.
* Must be approved by a department VP.

### 3. Local Travel and Mileage
* Mileage for business travel using personal vehicles is reimbursed at the standard government rate.
* Tolls and public transport expenses are reimbursable with receipt evidence.
* Standard daily commuting costs (home to office) are not reimbursable.

## Submission and Approval Process

1. **Submit Expense Report**:
   - Log in to the finance portal at `https://finance.generic-enterprise.com`.
   - Create a new expense report and fill in the merchant, date, amount, and business purpose for each line item.
   - Attach digital copies or scans of itemized receipts.

2. **Submission Deadline**:
   - Expense reports must be submitted within 30 days of incurring the expense. Late submissions may be rejected.

3. **Approval Flow**:
   - Reports under $500 are routed to your direct manager.
   - Reports $500 and above require additional approval from the department head.
   - Once approved by the manager/department head, the Finance team audits the report. Approved payments are processed via direct deposit within 7–10 business days.
