# Corporate Travel Booking Policy

This document outlines the guidelines and approvals required for booking business-related travel.

## Booking Channels and Tools
* **Preferred Tool**: All business flights, hotel accommodations, and car rentals must be booked through the company's preferred online booking tool at `https://travel.generic-enterprise.com`.
* **Direct Bookings**: Direct booking with airlines or hotels is not permitted unless the travel destination is not serviced by the corporate booking tool or pre-approved by the travel desk.

## Approval Guidelines

1. **Domestic Travel**:
   - Must be submitted and approved by your direct manager at least 14 days prior to departure.

2. **International Travel**:
   - Must be submitted and approved by your department VP at least 30 days prior to departure.

3. **Booking Class**:
   - **Air Travel**: Economy class is required for all flights under 6 hours. Business class may be requested only for continuous flights exceeding 6 hours with VP approval.
   - **Lodging**: Standard single room at preferred partner hotels. Room rates must comply with standard city-level budget caps.
   - **Car Rental**: Mid-size or compact car booking only. GPS and additional insurance options should be declined (covered by corporate credit card/policy).
