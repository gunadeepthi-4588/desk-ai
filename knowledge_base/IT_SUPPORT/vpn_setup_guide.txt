# VPN Setup and Configuration Guide

This guide provides instructions for configuring and troubleshooting the Virtual Network (VPN) connection for remote work.

## Prerequisites
Before configuring the VPN, ensure you have:
1. An active internet connection.
2. A corporate-managed device.
3. Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) set up on your mobile device.

## Connection Setup Steps
Follow these steps to configure your VPN connection:

1. **Open Network Settings**:
   - On Windows: Go to Settings > Network & Internet > VPN.
   - On macOS: Go to System Settings > Network > VPN.

2. **Add a VPN Connection**:
   - VPN Provider: Choose Windows (built-in) or the native macOS VPN client.
   - Connection Name: Corporate VPN.
   - Server Name or Address: `vpn.generic-enterprise.com`.
   - VPN Type: L2TP/IPsec with pre-shared key or IKEv2.
   - Type of sign-in info: User name and password.

3. **Authenticate**:
   - Enter your standard directory username and password.
   - Click "Save".

4. **Connect**:
   - Select the newly created "Corporate VPN" connection and click "Connect".
   - Approve the Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) prompt sent to your registered authenticator app.

## Troubleshooting Common Issues

### Issue 1: Connection Timeout / Server Unreachable
If the connection times out, perform the following troubleshooting steps:
* Verify your local Wi-Fi or Ethernet connection is active and stable.
* Ensure you are not behind a restrictive public firewall (e.g., hotel or coffee shop Wi-Fi) that blocks port 500 or 4500.
* Try restarting your home router or modem.

### Issue 2: Credentials Rejected
* Confirm that your primary directory account is not locked or expired.
* Verify you entered your full username without typos.
* Reset your password via the self-service portal if necessary.

### Issue 3: MFA Prompt Not Received
* Open your authenticator app manually to check for pending approvals.
* Ensure your mobile device has active cell service or Wi-Fi.
* If your device or phone number has changed, contact the Service Desk to update your MFA registration.
