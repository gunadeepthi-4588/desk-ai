# Password Reset Procedures

This document details the standard self-service and service desk procedures for resetting user directory passwords.

## Self-Service Password Reset (SSPR)
If you know your current password but want to change it, or if you have registered for self-service password recovery and forgot your password, use the SSPR portal:

1. Navigate to the recovery portal at `https://password.generic-enterprise.com`.
2. Enter your standard company email address or username.
3. Complete the verification challenge using your registered secondary authentication method (MFA push notification, SMS code, or personal email verification).
4. Enter and confirm your new password.

### Password Strength Requirements
Your new password must meet the following complexity criteria:
* Minimum length of 12 characters.
* Must include at least one uppercase letter (A-Z).
* Must include at least one lowercase letter (a-z).
* Must include at least one number (0-9).
* Must include at least one special character (e.g., !, @, #, $, %).
* Cannot contain your username or parts of your full name.
* Cannot match any of your previous 10 passwords.

## Service Desk Reset Procedure
If you are locked out of your account, have not registered for SSPR, or SSPR fails, you must contact the IT Service Desk:

1. Call the IT Support Hotline at extension 4357 (HELP) or submit a ticket via the web portal.
2. The technician will verify your identity using standard verification questions (employee ID, department, manager's name).
3. The technician will issue a temporary one-time password.
4. Log in to a workstation or web application using the temporary password. You will be prompted immediately to set a permanent password that complies with the complexity requirements.
